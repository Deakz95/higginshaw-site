Activities & Groups

Rugby League

Interested in being part of our successful Rugby League Team Higginshaw ARLFC? We have both Youth and Open Age teams, so get involved!

Contact Club Secretary John Mellor on 07970 100606 for more information.

Also check out our Rugby Fixtures page for more information, and results on our league fixtures, as well as our Kit Shop.

Premier Diamonds Dance Troupe

Love to dance or feel like there is an inner dancer within you? Come along and check out Premier Diamonds Dance Troupe.

Contact Lesley for further information on times and different age group sessions on 07769 182312.

Zumba

Want to get fit through dance & aerobic movement whilst enjoying the beat of salsa & latino music? Come along and try out one of our Zumba classes.

When? Friday evenings 6.30 – 7.30pm. (Post to be updated for timings)

Contact Sharon on 07707 265049 for more information or to book a session.

Darts, Dominoes & Crib Club

Fancy yourself at a game of darts, dominoes or crib and a new social scene to go with it?

You can find us in the Darts League too, currently ranking No.2! See for yourself on the Oldham Waterhead 501 League.

When? Monday evenings at 8.30pm.

Contact to book or for more information: John Mellor – 07970 100606

Pool

Got an eye for the ball? Get in touch to find out more about playing Pool in your local Community Centre.

Check us out featured in the Pool League here: oldhameastpoolleague.leaguerepublic.com

When? Tuesday evenings at 8.30pm.

Contact to book or for more information: John Mellor on 07970 100606.

Hire Our Venue For Your Event

Are you looking for a venue to host an event or occasion? Look no further than your local Community Centre, with the perfect facilities to make any event or occasion a memorable one.

See which dates are free and request a booking online, or call John Mellor on 07970 100606.
