Our New Website Is Live!

29 October 2026 · 2 min read

We’ve got a brand new home online: www.higginshawcommunityproject.uk

Our old website has retired, and everything has moved here. If you’ve saved our old address, please update your bookmarks to higginshawcommunityproject.uk.

What you’ll find

  • News: the latest updates from the club and the centre.
  • Activities: rugby league, dance, Zumba, darts, dominoes, crib and pool, with times and who to contact.
  • Rugby & Kit Shop: fixtures for Higginshaw ARLFC and our club kit.
  • Venue hire: our function room is available for parties, christenings, celebrations and classes.
  • Our history and press cuttings: over a century of Higginshaw rugby.

The new site works just as well on your phone or tablet as it does on a computer.

Get in touch

You can message us from the bottom of any page, and sign up there for community news by email. You’ll also find us on Facebook and YouTube.

Got news, photos or a result you’d like to see on here? Email us at higginshawcp@outlook.com.

All the best,
From The Higginshaw Community Project Team.
