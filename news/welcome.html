Welcome to Higginshaw Community Project

15 November 2022 · 1 min read · Updated 6 January 2023

Welcome to Higginshaw Community Project......

We are delighted to have finally launched our very own website promoting all of the various activities we have going on within Higginshaw Community Project, here in the heart of our beloved town, Oldham.

Whether it's rugby, or dance, darts or dominoes, there's something for everyone. A great social venue that we are proud to have been involved in the development of over many years, to provide a space that belongs to the community for all occasions, as well as activities, and brings people together.

Activity or Occasion?

Higginshaw Community Project has an array of activities available, but is also a space that is available to hire for any occasion. Be it a birthday party, christening or wedding, we have a beautiful high spec space, that can cater for your needs. Simply get in touch to find out more on costs and availability, and any other questions you may have.

Are You A Service Provider Looking For A Venue To Promote Your Services?

Then look no further. If you are an instructor or facilitator of an activity that you are interested in utilising the space for to host a weekly class, or event, then please do get in touch now to see how you can utilise our venue, and reach more people with the services or activity that you provide. There's people waiting for it! So don't delay.

We look forward to seeing many of your faces walk through our doors, whether it's to join a club or class, or for an occasion that marks a milestone, we welcome you gladly to Higginshaw Community Project. :)

All the best,
From The Higginshaw Community Project Team.
