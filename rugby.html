Higginshaw ARLFC – Our Local Triumphant Team.

Junior Rugby League was first played by Higginshaw when the Oldham League was formed in 1896. The successes of our club over the years have never been chronicled, but the name of Higginshaw ARLFC is covered in glory.

We have won League Championships at various levels, appeared in 22 Standard Cup Finals (13 Wins), achieved three Lancashire Cup successes and have reached the first round proper of the Rugby League Challenge Cup on four occasions.

Interested in playing? Then get in touch now!

Interested In Sponsoring Us?

Higginshaw ARLFC is delighted to announce our partnership with SNAP Sponsorship.

SNAP is specifically designed for aiding the development of UK-based grassroots sports clubs, providing them with an accessible online platform where users can promote sponsorship opportunities and manage commercial partners.

SNAP has given us the ability to design and execute sponsorship packages that deliver tangible value to our existing and future partners and in doing so maximising return for sponsors and enhance the club we all love.
